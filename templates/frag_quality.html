What FQC rejected, and what it is — GY or BGY

ⓘFQC records pass or rejected. A pass is grade A. What is rejected has no grade at all until it is called here, and no grade is what keeps it out of a box — packing wants a graded module whose grade matches the label.
The call is made from the same three things every time: the Sun Simulator reading, the EL verdict and its image, and what FQC recorded — the defect, the coded reason if the operator overruled the proposal, and the note.
