Coverage gaps

⚑ Hi-Pot is off-LAN. Every FQC record carries NOT_AVAILABLE for it. There is deliberately no "Hi-Pot done" checkbox — an unverifiable tick is worse than an honest blank.
⚑ EL and VI folder names are dirty at source (case variants, double spaces, "Buring" for "Burning"). They are normalised to codes on ingest and never stored raw.
ⓘThe grade rule table is versioned data, not code. FQC proposes a grade from the version in force at the moment of scanning, and that version is stamped onto the record — so a rule change today cannot silently restate why a module was graded last month.
