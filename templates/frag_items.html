The back label is the controlled document saying what may legally be built and labelled, so the master is seeded from it — not from what has been produced. The serial embeds the wattage, so a missing item blocks serial generation on the morning production starts. That has happened once already: ISEN630-G12R shipped on a real challan while it was absent.
