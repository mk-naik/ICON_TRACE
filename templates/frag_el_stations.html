Pre-EL, Post EL and Final EL · every module imaged, its rework, its rejections

ⓘOK is Pass; any other folder is NG. At Pre-EL an NG module goes for rework and comes back until it passes. After lamination a module cannot be reworked, so an NG at Post EL takes it off the line to Quality, who decide (Needs Review). Final EL is FQC’s own EL: its newest image is what FQC reads, and an NG there is FQC’s to decide. A module counts on the factory day (06:00 to 06:00) and shift of its first image at a station.
